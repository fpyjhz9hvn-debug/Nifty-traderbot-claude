Market regime — the "not today" gate

This app only ever buys. A long-only breakout system takes its worst losses when the whole market is rolling over and every breakout fails. Distribution days and participation make that visible before the trade, and the Risk Manager blocks new entries when this says so.

Circuit breaker & heat

Loss limits on realised damage, a cooldown after consecutive losses, and a cap on total open risk. Getting out is never blocked by any of it.

Your expectancy

win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss. Positive and there is an edge so far; negative and sizing up only loses faster.

Does any of this actually work?

The setup re-run over every session in the 60-day pool, across the universe, with and without the regime gate — same entries, same exits, so the comparison is fair. Takes about half a minute.

Seven setups, one honest test

Seven long-only setups, all written down before anything ran, over the same sessions. The older 60% of sessions are the design half and the newer 40% the confirm half: a setup counts only if it is positive in both, on 20+ trades each, and still positive after charges. Every setup is shown, including the losers.
